Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how ReclaimIt ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares your information when you use our mobile application. By using ReclaimIt, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Account information: email address, first and last name (via Clerk authentication)
  • Photos: images you upload when reporting lost or found items
  • Messages: chat conversations with other users about item returns
  • Location: approximate location where an item was lost or found (provided by you)

Information Collected Automatically

  • Device type, operating system version, and unique device identifiers
  • App usage statistics (features used, crash reports)
  • IP address and access times (server logs)

How We Use Your Information

  • To connect lost items with their owners through matchmaking
  • To facilitate chat communication between users regarding item returns
  • To display item listings publicly within the app
  • To send notifications about matches, messages, and claim status
  • To improve the app, fix bugs, and analyze usage patterns
  • To enforce our Terms of Service and block banned users

Third-Party Services We Use

  • Clerk — authentication and user management
  • Cloudinary — image hosting and delivery
  • MongoDB Atlas — database hosting
  • Google Gemini — AI-powered item matching

Each of these services has its own privacy policy governing how they handle data. We only share the minimum data necessary for each service to function.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share your information:

  • With other users as necessary for the Service (e.g., your chat messages are visible to the other participant)
  • With third-party service providers listed above
  • If required by law or to protect rights and safety

Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active. After account deletion, we may retain limited data for up to 24 months to comply with legal obligations or resolve disputes. Chat transcripts are retained up to 24 months for quality assurance. Server logs are retained up to 24 months for security purposes.

Your Rights
